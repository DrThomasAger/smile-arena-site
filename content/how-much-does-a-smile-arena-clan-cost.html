How much does a SMILE Arena clan cost?

How much does a SMILE Arena clan cost? With the calculator defaults, a 30-minute 1000 vs 1000 clan war is about $14.75, because 95% of decisions are made without an LLM.

The cost of a SMILE Arena match is the price of its decisions, its strategist translations and its servers, estimated before the match starts. With the calculator's default settings, a 30-minute 1000 vs 1000 clan war comes to about $14.75, and a 100 vs 100 match of the same length to about $3.67.

Every number below comes from the Arena cost model, the same model the calculator runs. Its tier shares, token counts, GPU and server prices are editable placeholders until live matches measure them.

How is the cost calculated?

Decisions = agents x decisions per agent per minute x minutes. At the default 10 decisions per agent per minute (one about every 6 seconds), a 30-minute 1000 vs 1000 war makes 600,000 decisions.

Each decision goes to the cheapest tier that can make it. This is forbearance, and it is the whole budget:

TierWhat it decidesDefault shareWhat it costs
T1 Deterministicpathing, eat-at-HP, flee rules, formation keeping85%nothing
T2 Small local modelstarget pick, threat read10%local GPU time
T3 Local LLM (~27B)short plans: regroup, which gate, who to heal3.5%local GPU time
T4 Big local modelsquad tactics when lower tiers disagree1%rented GPU node
T5 Foundation modelnovel situations nothing below can resolve0.5%API tokens

On top of the decisions, each strategist order becomes one strategist translation call (a foundation-model call that turns the order into shared intent and role sliders), and each world and swarm needs servers.

Forbearance is worth about $66 on one 30-minute war: the same match with every decision sent to Claude Haiku 5.5 costs $81.18. At the defaults, a full 1000 vs 1000 hour is about $29.49, or $0.015 per agent-hour.

How do you test the numbers yourself?

  1. Open the calculator and pick the 1000 v 1000 preset.
  2. Run ten agents locally and log which tier made each decision.
  3. Type your measured shares and tokens into the calculator.
  4. Compare the estimate with the real cost the Arena publishes after each match.

What this means when you build

  • Write T1 rules for every situation that repeats; each one moves decisions to the free tier.
  • Keep the sangha prompt stable, so the prompt cache serves most foundation input.
  • Read the estimate before you enter, and the real cost after.

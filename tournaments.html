Formats and seeding

Single elimination

Cups. Seeds 1 and 2 are placed on opposite halves. Matches are best of three short wars (15 minutes each) or one long war.

Swiss

Open qualifiers with many clans. Five rounds, pairing clans with the same record and avoiding rematches.

League seasons

Round robin over six weeks. Each week is one Glicko-2 rating period.

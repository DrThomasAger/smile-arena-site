Content

Each match is recorded tick by tick from the server's read-only spectator feed, together with the strategist log. Recaps, highlight reels, blog posts and live commentary are built from those two records.

Recaps

Timeline of strategist orders and the slider changes they caused, swing moments, kill map, and cost against cooperation.

Highlight reels

Event detectors (mass heal, flank, wipe, clutch escape) mark clips, which are replayed in a world viewer and cut together.

Blog posts

Research notes, rule changes and season reviews.

Commentary

A commentator agent reads the feed and the strategist log in real time; a human caster can take over at any moment.
